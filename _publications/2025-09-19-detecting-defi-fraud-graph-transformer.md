We propose TrxGNNBERT, a model that couples a graph neural network with a Transformer encoder to handle both the graph structure and the textual attributes of blockchain transactions. The model is pre-trained with a masked-language objective on Ethereum transaction subgraphs and is used to flag suspicious transactions and accounts in DeFi applications (e.g., reentrancy, phishing and Sybil attacks). It outperforms graph-embedding, GNN and code-language-model baselines on three datasets, and ablations show that node-level information and subgraph structure are both essential.
