I am a second-year PhD student at the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), Nanyang Technological University (NTU), where I am very lucky to be supervised by [Prof. Yang Liu](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/yangliu/). I entered Wuhan University in September 2021 and received my bachelor's degree from the School of Cyber Science and Engineering in June 2025, under the supervision of [Prof. Jing Chen](https://cse.whu.edu.cn/info/3421/37531.htm). I joined NTU in August 2025.

Research interests
======
My current research focuses on **large language model (LLM)-driven domain modeling and requirements engineering**, and on applying them to downstream code-related tasks. Outcomes of this research have been applied in scenarios including automotive (in-vehicle) software, enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems, and game generation.

Before joining NTU, my research centered on **blockchain security**, including DeFi security and Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus for consortium blockchains.
